## 👋 About Me

<img align="right" alt="Coding" width="360" src="https://cdn.dribbble.com/users/1162077/screenshots/3848914/programmer.gif" />

I'm a **Full Stack Developer** passionate about bringing ideas to life and turning them into practical applications.
I grew up coding with **JavaScript** and **PHP**, and today **React**, **React Native**, and **Laravel** are my magic toolbox for building anything I imagine.

Lately I've been diving deep into **AI** and **ONNX**, running machine learning models right inside web and mobile apps — from **browser-based inference** to **on-device intelligence**.

For me, coding is more than just a job — it's a chance to solve complex puzzles, collaborate with creative teams, and never stop learning.
